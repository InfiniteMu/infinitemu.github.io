I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student in Economics at Johns Hopkins University. My research interests are in microeconomic theory, especially questions about incentives, information, and strategic decision-making. I like models that clarify how people, firms, and institutions behave when their choices depend on what others know, believe, or do.
